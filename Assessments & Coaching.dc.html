SOLUTIONS — ASSESSMENTS & COACHING

Assessments & Coaching

Organizations improve performance not through training alone, but through a clear understanding of their people, their strengths, and the gaps that stand in the way of growth.

Impact combines leadership assessments, diagnostics, and coaching to help organizations uncover capability gaps, translate insight into action, and accelerate leadership development and performance.

Our Assessment Approach

Impact integrates assessment insights into leadership development journeys, ensuring that insight is directly translated into targeted action. By combining assessment data, simulations, and structured coaching interventions, organizations can identify development priorities, activate leadership capability, strengthen pipelines, and drive sustained performance improvement.

What Organizations Gain

Organizations that integrate assessments and coaching into their leadership development strategy gain clearer insights into leadership potential, stronger talent pipelines, and more targeted and effective development initiatives. These insights translate into better decision-making, more focused development, and measurable improvement in leadership capability and organizational performance.

When Organizations Use Assessments

Organizations typically integrate assessments into leadership development and talent strategies when they need clearer insights into individual strengths, leadership potential, and team dynamics. Assessments are commonly used in situations such as:

Leadership Development Programs — understanding leadership strengths and development areas before launching development journeys.

Talent Identification & Succession Planning — identifying high-potential employees and preparing future leaders.

Team Development Initiatives — understanding team dynamics, communication styles, and collaboration patterns.

Entrepreneurial & Innovation Programs — identifying individuals with strong entrepreneurial potential.

Coaching Engagements — providing leaders with deeper insights into their leadership style and behavioral patterns, enabling more focused and effective coaching conversations.
